1. The #1 Battle: Florida vs. Duke Top Tier Separation

Florida (4-0, SEC) holds the #1 Consensus Rank (+31.59 Net Rtg), ranking #1 in KenPom (+34.08), #1 in EvanMiya Regular (+33.8), #1 in Warren ELO (1717.4), and #2 in ESPN BPI (22.2). However, in Live Rankings, Duke (3-1, ACC) takes #1 (+40.30 Live Net vs. Florida's +40.11) because Florida carries a -1.03% EvanMiya injury delta on offense while Duke is at full strength (0.0% delta) with the nation's #1 Consensus Defense (84.30 DRtg).

2. Power Conference Hierarchy SEC / B12 / B10 Tri-opoly

The SEC leads all 31 conferences in average Consensus Net Rating (+18.36 across 16 teams) with 6 teams in the Top 25 (Florida #1, Texas #9, LSU #14, Kentucky #15, Tennessee #16, Auburn #22). The Big 12 (+16.88 avg, 6 Top-25 teams led by Houston #6, Kansas #7, Arizona #8, Texas Tech #11) and Big Ten (+16.09 avg, 6 Top-25 teams led by Illinois #3, Michigan St. #4, UCLA #13, Purdue #18, Michigan #20) form a tight three-conference tier ahead of the Big East (+15.03) and ACC (+13.74).

3. Critical Injury Deltas Reshaping Lines EvanMiya + KP Injury Blend

Your spreadsheet's Injury Delta column pinpoints where roster availability diverges from full-strength ratings: Texas Tech (-2.97% delta, dropping from #11 Consensus to #19 Live), Illinois (-2.69% delta, #3 Consensus to #5 Live despite the #1 offense at 117.28 ORtg), Boise St. (-1.89%, #64 to #72), Memphis (-1.63%, #87 to #91), TCU (-1.52%, #45 to #59), and Clemson (-1.43%, #70 to #87).
