1. The #1 Battle: Florida vs. Duke Top Tier Separation

Florida (4-0, SEC) holds the #1 Consensus Rank (+31.59 Net Rtg), ranking #1 in KenPom (+34.08), #1 in EvanMiya Regular (+33.8), #1 in Warren ELO (1717.3), and #2 in ESPN BPI (22.2). However, in Live Rankings, Duke (3-1, ACC) takes #1 (+40.30 Live Net vs. Florida's +40.11) because Florida carries a -1.03% EvanMiya injury delta on offense while Duke is at full strength (0.0% delta) with the nation's #1 Consensus Defense (84.30 DRtg).

2. Power Conference Hierarchy SEC / B12 / B10 Tri-opoly

The SEC leads all conferences in average Consensus Net Rating (+18.36) with deep Top-25 strength (Florida, Texas, Vanderbilt, Alabama, Tennessee, Texas A&M). The Big 12 (Houston, Kansas, Arizona, Texas Tech, Iowa St., BYU) and Big Ten (Illinois, Michigan St., UCLA, Nebraska, Michigan, USC) form a tight three-conference tier ahead of the Big East and ACC.

3. Critical Injury Deltas Reshaping Lines EvanMiya + KP Injury Blend

The Injury Delta column pinpoints where roster availability diverges from full-strength ratings: Texas Tech (-2.97% delta, dropping from #11 Consensus to #19 Live), Illinois (-2.69% delta, #3 Consensus to #5 Live despite the #1 offense at 117.28 ORtg), and Florida (-1.03% delta).
