Current Climate of College Basketball: Macro Insights & Live Matchup Simulator

Synthesizing power conference hierarchy, injury-adjusted rating shifts, tempo/size extremes, and workbook diagnostics across all 365 Division I teams and 488 graded simulations.

1. The #1 Battle: Florida vs. Duke Top Tier Separation

Florida (4-0, SEC) holds the #1 Consensus Rank (+31.59 Net Rtg), ranking #1 in KenPom (+34.08), #1 in EvanMiya Regular (+33.8), #1 in Warren ELO (1717.4), and #2 in ESPN BPI (22.2). However, in Live Rankings, Duke (3-1, ACC) takes #1 (+40.30 Live Net vs. Florida's +40.11) because Florida carries a -1.03% EvanMiya injury delta on offense while Duke is at full strength (0.0% delta) with the nation's #1 Consensus Defense (84.30 DRtg).

2. Power Conference Hierarchy SEC / B12 / B10 Tri-opoly

The SEC leads all conferences in average Consensus Net Rating (+18.36 across 16 teams) with 6 teams in the Top 25 (Florida #1, Texas #9, Vanderbilt #14, Alabama #19, Tennessee #21, Texas A&M #23). The Big 12 (+16.88 avg) and Big Ten (+16.09 avg) form a tight three-conference tier ahead of the Big East (+15.03) and ACC (+13.74).

3. Critical Injury Deltas Reshaping Lines EvanMiya + KP Injury Blend

Your Injury Delta column pinpoints where roster availability diverges from full-strength ratings: Texas Tech (-2.97% delta, dropping from #11 Consensus to #19 Live), Illinois (-2.69% delta, #3 Consensus to #5 Live despite the #1 offense at 117.28 ORtg), Boise St. (-1.89%), Memphis (-1.63%), TCU (-1.52%), and Clemson (-1.43%).

4. Simulation Engine Climate & Formula Audit 488 Graded Games

Across 488 graded games (Nov 5–15), Adj. Eff. leads overall winner accuracy at 67.01% (327/488) and TEAM AND DIFF (21.93%), closely followed by Live Morey Z at 66.80% (326/488), which leads all models in hitting full ALL outcomes (11.48%, 56/488). Meanwhile, Base is ultra-conservative in assigning 90%+ confidence—picking only 90 games vs. 192–224 for Morey/Log5, but hitting 91.11% (82/90) when it does.
